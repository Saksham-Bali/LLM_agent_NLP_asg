## Matchup Overview
The matchup is between **Lux** (Mid lane, AP damage type) and **Zed**. The difficulty rating for this matchup is **Hard** 🚨. In summary, the Lux vs Zed matchup is challenging for Lux, as Zed's agility and burst damage can make it difficult for her to land her abilities. However, if Lux can manage to stay safe and scale into the mid game, she can become a formidable opponent 💪.

## Power Spikes
Here's a breakdown of Lux's power spikes in the game:
* **Early Game**: Lux's early game is focused on harassing Zed with her E and passive, while also trying to deny him gold and experience. She can use her Q to last-hit minions and zone Zed away from the wave.
* **Mid Game**: As Lux reaches level 6, she gains access to her ultimate, which can be a game-changer in team fights. She can use it to burst down Zed or lock him down for her allies to take him out.
* **Late Game**: In the late game, Lux's power spikes come from her ability to roam effectively and deal damage to Zed from a distance. She can use her E to slow him down and her R to finish him off.
Key levels: 6, 11, 16
Key items: Luden's Tempest, Rabadon's Deathcap
